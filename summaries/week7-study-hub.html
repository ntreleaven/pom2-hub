    Musculoskeletal Medicine

    Five modules covering the shared vocabulary of the MSK system, then applying it to the two most common MSK complaints — axial spine pain and shoulder pain. Starts with anatomy review and movement/joint terminology, moves through mechanical and non-mechanical back & neck pain, the systematic read of a spine X-ray, and finishes with the osteology and joints of the proximal upper extremity. Read a section, then clear its quiz before moving on.
