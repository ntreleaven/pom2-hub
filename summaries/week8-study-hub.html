    Musculoskeletal Medicine

    Eight modules that finish the shoulder and carry the upper limb down to the hand: the brachial plexus, the muscles of the shoulder and arm with rotator cuff disease and biceps tendinopathy, the neurovascular supply of the arm and forearm, the elbow (joints, ligaments, bursa and tendons), ulnar neuropathy, and the wrist and hand with distal radius and scaphoid fractures.
